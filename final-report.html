When the pings went quiet.

Release 4.2 changed who was prioritized and shortened the time to answer. Fewer pings were taken, misses rose, and handlers reported quiet phones. The precise cause remains unproven.

Investigation → prototype → review habit
01 / The release

Two changes. One difficult signal.

90 → 60 sec

Universal wait before an unanswered ping became a miss.

2 → 38

Missed pings: the week before release versus the week of August 10, which straddled the August 12 release.

The ranking and timeout changed together. Seasonality, availability and other factors also matter; the timing alone does not identify a cause.

05 / The habit · module 6

A better brief before the next release.

The review-checklist skill checks four questions every Monday morning. It can expose gaps early; it cannot by itself prevent a routing defect or replace testing and evidence.

01 · Who owns it?

Names a person or role accountable for the work.

02 · How will we know?

Defines observable outcomes and measures of success.

03 · Is scope consistent?

Checks that the problem, deliverables and measures match.

04 · Problem before fix?

Ensures the need and context are clear before the solution.

Exact saved prompt · Module 6can you run one for today

The review found that the Module 5 brief explains the problem, but lacks an accountable owner and success measures; its scope remains unclear because the proposed work is not defined in the brief.
