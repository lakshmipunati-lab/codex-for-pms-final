**A plausible mechanism is a self-reinforcing drop in routing priority.** In the available routing code, a missed ping lowers a responder’s recent-acceptance score just as an intentional decline does. A lower score can put the responder later in the queue; if someone ahead takes the callout, she receives no ping and therefore has fewer opportunities to take one and raise her score. The code does not show the score recovering simply because time passes. This is a plausible explanation for Meteor Mite’s quiet period, not a demonstrated cause. **How Rook distinguishes the three situations**

| Situation | Recorded meaning | Effect in the shown routing flow |
|---|---|---|
| **Missed** | No answer before the ping wait expires. | Moves to the next responder and subtracts 0.12 from recent acceptance. |
| **Turned down** | The responder explicitly says no. | Recorded separately from a miss, but moves on and receives the same 0.12 penalty. |
| **Temporarily unavailable** | The responder or handler sets availability. | A person marked unavailable is outside the available pool for that time; the shown flow does not generate a missed ping or score penalty merely for that status. |

The distinction between miss and decline exists in the outcome data, but the shown scoring logic collapses them into the same “no yes” treatment. A miss alone does not tell Rook *why* there was no answer. Availability is a separate stated status; the implementation of the availability lookup is a placeholder, so its deployed behavior needs confirmation. **What remains an assumption**

The history does not establish that Meteor Mite was available and eligible for each unoffered callout, where she ranked, or that her score caused the change. The database lacks eligible-pool and ranking traces, as well as delivery/open timestamps. Proximity, the shorter timeout, availability, other responders’ acceptance, and changing callout volume could also contribute. Priya cautioned that August seasonality and the simultaneous timeout change made the release’s effect hard to isolate. The shown score is held in memory, with no durable storage visible, and phone delivery and availability functions are placeholders.
