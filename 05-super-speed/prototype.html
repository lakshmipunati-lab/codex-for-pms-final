What may be happening to Meteor Mite

A starting point for a conversation, not a diagnosis of Meteor Mite’s routing.

What we can seeMeteor Mite was offered 9 of 57 Eastgate callouts after August 12, compared with 62 of 114 before August 12. After her August 16 missed ping, she received no offer on the next five Eastgate callouts.
A possible cycle for Meteor MiteWhen Meteor Mite misses an offer, the shown routing code lowers her recent-acceptance score, just as it does when she turns one down. That could place her later in line. If someone ahead of her accepts, her phone may not receive an offer, giving her fewer chances to take one and improve that score. We don’t know whether this caused her quiet period.
What Meteor Mite’s outcomes meanA miss means Meteor Mite did not answer before the wait expired; it does not tell us why. A decline means she explicitly said no. Both have the same score effect in the shown code. Her availability is set separately by her or Kip.
What Kip can’t see yetWe need Meteor Mite’s eligibility, availability and ranking traces to explain individual callouts. Release 4.2 also shortened the wait and changed proximity weighting. Her available status above is illustrative.
