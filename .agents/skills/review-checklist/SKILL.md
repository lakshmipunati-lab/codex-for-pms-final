# Review checklist

Read the complete brief before reviewing it. Identify the relevant passages and check whether it:

1. **Names an owner:** Identifies a person or role accountable for the brief or initiative. Contributors alone do not establish ownership.
2. **Defines success:** Explains how the team will know the work succeeded, using observable measures or outcomes and, where appropriate, a target or timeframe.
3. **Keeps a consistent scope:** Describes the same boundaries from beginning to end. Flag additions, omissions, or contradictions between the problem, proposed work, deliverables, and success measures.
4. **Explains the problem before the fix:** States the user or business problem and its context before presenting the proposed solution, with enough detail to understand why the work is needed.

Report each criterion as **Pass**, **Needs work**, or **Unclear**, with concise evidence from the brief. For each issue, explain what is missing or inconsistent and suggest a specific improvement. Do not invent owners, targets, scope decisions, or evidence; identify them as questions for the author. If the user requests edits, preserve the brief's intent and make only supported changes.
